Hi everyone, how are you? I'm Zoe Quintanar and welcome to today's kitchen. Today I want to talk about something that happens to all of us at home. It's about sweet bread. The first trick: keep it in a cloth bag, never in plastic.

The second trick is to keep it out of the fridge, because it dries out faster in there. The third trick is to warm it in a pan before you eat it. That way yesterday's sweet bread is soft again. If you have a lot left, freeze it the same day.
